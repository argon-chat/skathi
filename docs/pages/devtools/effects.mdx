Shader effects are hard to tune by editing numbers in a stylesheet: is the glass too thick at `22px` or at `28px`, is a dispersion of `.2` enough? The **Effects** tab answers that with sliders, on the element itself, while the app runs.

## Tune an effect

1. **Pick the element.** The left column lists **In use**: every element that paints an effect through `--shading`, with the declaration it has now. Effects that animate are marked. Select one.
2. **Move the sliders.** The editor shows the effect's description, its signature, and one control per parameter: a slider and a number for lengths and numbers, a colour picker with an alpha slider for colours, and a switch for an optional parameter. Each change applies to the element in the app at once, and the element is marked **edited**.
3. **Copy the result.** The **CSS** box shows the resulting declaration, such as `--shading: lens(22px, 32px, 0.45, #ffffff0d);`, and under it what it **was** and the rule it came from. **Copy** puts it on the clipboard for your stylesheet; **Reset** drops the change.

A value out of an effect's range is clamped, and the editor says so under the CSS box.

The **Available** list below shows every effect the app can use, built-in or its own, with flags for what each one does, such as **reads backdrop**, **reads content**, **animated** or **bleeds** past the element's box. Select one there to see its controls, and press **Apply** to put it on the selected element.

The change lives only in the running app; it does not touch your files.
